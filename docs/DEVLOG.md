## 2026-10-19/20 - Finishing the shards

**Order of work.** Cinder, Slipstream, Masks and Orrery's galaxy core landed first; the remaining shards were built one after another.

**Orrery** (scenes, station and decode screens, codex, civsim fixes), **Concord** (hex campaign, supply, diplomacy with explained AI acceptance, tactical battles, bounded AI adaptation, fog memory, save fidelity), **Terrace** (512x256 world, light, conservative fluids, ecology, structures, edited-chunk persistence), **Umbra** (BSP levels with a solvability proof, noise/light stealth, three senses, director, shadow-casting renderer) were each built to the same recipe: pure simulation first, tests for the rules, then scene/UI, then headless screenshots read as images, then performance assertions. Notable bugs found by tests: Orrery civsim hash and arbitrage, Concord rounding in saves and a charge test, Terrace herd explosion and an unwinnable keystone site, Umbra unsolvable levels (keys behind their own locks) and an infinite BSP split.

**Verdant** (this entry). `worldgen.py`/`landmarks.py` already existed with no data; added 14 landmark templates (composed by `tools/gen_verdant_templates.py`, shipped as JSON), `interiors.py` (house layouts and validated seeded dungeons), the simulation (`sim.py`, `combat.py`, `npcs.py`, `quests.py`, `actions.py`), art and scenes, 27 effects, and 8 JSON content kinds (21 NPCs, 11 quests, 39 items, 11 creatures, 9 shops, 14 recipes, 9 loot tables). Bugs found by the new tests/tools: `np.cross` on 2-D vectors (removed in NumPy 2) in crossing selection; the starting sword was not in the pack so it vanished after a save/load; the inn's rest healed to the *feverish* maximum; shop caravan goods ignored the unlock predicate; leaving through the shared pause menu did not save.

**Audio pipeline** (`tools/gen_audio.py`): first real render found an off-by-one in `core/music.py::room_ir` that broke every track, and 13 sound recipes in Cinder, Terrace, Umbra and Verdant that had been failing silently at run time. All fixed; 47 MB of WAVs and a hashed manifest rendered to `assets/audio` (git-ignored, regenerated by `tools/gen_audio.py`); tests added.

**Docs and verification.** STATUS, KNOWN_ISSUES, AUDIO, ASSETS and per-shard pages written from what the tests actually assert; `tools/contact_sheet.py` produces real-gameplay screenshots of every shard; `tools/perf.py` now starts real gameplay instead of measuring menus; `tests/test_echo_web.py` runs the nine-link cross-shard chain through the real echo engine and checks that every unlock an echo grants has a consumer; the sample mod now exercises Verdant and Umbra kinds.
